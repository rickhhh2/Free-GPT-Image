# Free GPT Image — Free AI Image Generator

[FreeGPTImage.com](https://freegptimage.com/) is a browser-based AI image generator that brings together today's top image models — **GPT Image 2**, **Nano Banana 2**, and more — in a single workspace. Describe what you want in any language, optionally drop in reference photos, and get studio-quality images in seconds. Free, no install, no credit card.

## What it is

Most people don't want to juggle five different AI tools just to make one picture. Free GPT Image is a small project built around a simple idea: put the best image models in one place, keep it free to try, and stay out of your way.

You can use it for:

- Posters, social posts, and thumbnails
- Product mockups and ads
- Wallpapers, icons, and concept art
- Style remixes of your own photos (Ghibli, crayon, anime, photoreal, etc.)

## Features

- **Multiple top-tier models** — GPT Image 2, Nano Banana 2, and other leading text-to-image models in one dropdown.
- **Text-to-image & image-to-image** — generate from a prompt, or upload up to 10 reference images to remix a style or edit a subject.
- **Up to 4K, any aspect ratio** — render at 1K / 2K / 4K, choose ratios from 1:1 to 21:9.
- **Prompt in any language** — English, 한국어, 日本語, Deutsch, Español, 中文 — describe it the way you'd say it out loud.
- **Smart prompt enhancer** — one click rewrites your idea with stronger composition, lighting, and style cues. Off by default if you'd rather drive the prompt yourself.
- **Built-in editing** — upscale to 4K, erase backgrounds, inpaint, outpaint — finish the image without leaving the tab.
- **Personal gallery** — every render is saved, searchable by prompt, and re-runnable with one tap.
- **Runs anywhere** — 100% browser-based on phone, tablet, or laptop. No GPU, no install.

## How it works

1. **Pick a model.** GPT Image 2, Nano Banana 2, or another from the list.
2. **Describe your image.** Plain words, any language. Add reference photos if you want.
3. **Choose the look.** Aspect ratio, resolution, and how many variations (1–4) to render at once.
4. **Generate & download.** Most images arrive in under a minute. Hit "Create similar" to keep iterating.

## Which model should I pick?

A rough rule of thumb based on what the community keeps reporting:

- **GPT Image 2** — clean photorealism, product shots, and accurate in-image text and typography.
- **Nano Banana 2** — more cinematic and stylized output, strong for posters and editorial looks.
- **T2I (general text-to-image)** — quick drafts and exploration when you're not sure of the direction yet.

When in doubt, run the same prompt through two models and keep the one you like.

## FAQ

**Is it really free?**
Yes. You get 10 credits at signup and a daily free quota that refreshes. No trial timer, no card required.

**Do I need to install anything?**
No. It runs in any modern browser. Sign in with Google in one tap if you want to save your gallery.

**Can I use the images commercially?**
Commercial use is included on paid plans (full rights, no watermarks). Free-tier renders are intended for personal, non-commercial use; you can upgrade later to unlock commercial rights on past images too.

**Why doesn't my output match what I described?**
Try the prompt enhancer, add a reference image, and describe lighting / composition / style — not just the subject. Generating 2–4 variations at once usually nails the look faster.

**Is my data private?**
Your gallery is private to your account. Reference images you upload are used only to generate your image.
